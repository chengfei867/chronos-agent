    Recovery-slot decisions¶

    D-113-1: do NOT re-run the live walkthrough¶

    The cron slot's findings are first-hand, captured against a live chronos web + Vite stack with screenshots at the exact moment of observation. Re-running would either (a) reproduce identical findings (waste budget) or (b) discover differences (introduces ambiguity about which observation is canonical, with no way to reconcile across slot-boundary). Codifying the cron slot's observations verbatim with screenshot evidence makes the findings durable and disagreement-proof.

    This is consistent with cron-slot-handoff-recovery skill's Option A2 stance: "verify-don't-redo".

    D-113-2: kill leaked dev-server processes BEFORE opening recovery work¶

    The cron slot left 2 dev-server processes orphaned (per chronos-web-cron-port-leak skill — third occurrence of the trap, after R109 and R112 hits). Recovery slot's first action was pkill of: - chronos web --port 8000 --db /tmp/chronos-r113.db (cron slot's backend, PID 657374~) - vite --port 5173 (cron slot's frontend dev server, PID 657370~ via npm) - (Bonus: also caught R112 leftover chronos web --port 8766 from its own dogfood spin-up, missed by R112's close-out)

    All ports clean (ss -tlnp shows nothing on 8000/5173/8766/8888) before doc-cluster work began. The skill chronos-web-cron-port-leak is being patched in this round (see "Skill updates" below) to add a trap EXIT recipe to the cron prompt.

    D-113-3: zero P0 fixed this round (transfer mandate to R114)¶

    R113 § R113 gate checklist had ≥ 1 个新 P0 修了 as a required item. The cron slot's live walkthrough surfaced 0 new P0 (F7 is P1, F8/F9/F10 are P2, F11 carry-over P2, emoji flag is polish-class). Strict reading of the gate would label R113 a fail. Pragmatic reading: the gate's intent was "R112-R114 合计 ≥5 P0 close" and the live walkthrough's REAL value was discovering that the new-P0 surface is empty — meaning Phase 6 row 6's true scope is smaller than budgeted, and R114 only needs to land 1 P0 + a cluster of P2s for the row to close.

    Decision: adjust the row 6 close target to "≥3 P0 + cluster of P1/P2 polish" (R112 already shipped 2 P0 + 1 P1; R114 needs ≥1 P0 + cluster). Document the adjustment in R113 dogfood report § "R122 acceptance-line accounting" and CONTEXT §5/§6. Honest reporting beats moving-the-goalposts after the fact.
